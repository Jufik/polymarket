# Profitability Distribution Analysis

> Polymarket trader profitability across 70.9M trader-market positions.
> Data covers 2022-11 through 2026-01.

## Key Takeaways

1. **Most traders lose money.** Only 46.5% of 2.08M traders are net profitable. The median PnL is -$0.20 -- essentially breakeven, but the mean is -$189, dragged down by heavy losers.

2. **The house always wins.** Aggregate PnL across all traders is **-$393M** on $41.7B total volume, implying a ~0.94% fee drag. Losers (-$3.53B) far exceed winners (+$3.13B).

3. **Extreme concentration.** The top 1% of traders by volume account for 78.8% of all volume. The top 50 earners made $395M total; the bottom 50 lost $858M.

4. **Mid-tier volume is the sweet spot.** Traders in the $10K-$1M volume range are the most likely to be profitable (52-54%). The $1M+ tier flips negative -- large traders face adverse selection and fee drag at scale.

5. **Win rate matters, but not how you think.** Profitable traders need only a ~53% win rate (median). But even 60-80% win rate traders average only $548 profit -- edge is thin.

6. **Takers outperform makers.** Heavy takers (<20% MVF) with $10K+ volume have 57.9% profitability and +$7,433 avg PnL. Heavy makers (80%+ MVF) have 49.6% profitability and -$23,507 avg PnL. Market making on Polymarket appears to be a losing proposition for most participants.

7. **Explosive growth.** Active traders grew from ~200/month (early 2023) to 575K/month (Jan 2026). Monthly volume peaked at ~$6B in Jan 2026.
